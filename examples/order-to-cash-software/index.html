A software order is not a goods order

There is no delivery and no goods issue. The signed order form is the order. Activating the licenses takes the place of the delivery, billing follows the contract, and revenue is earned over the term.

Two tracks at order confirmation

The commercial track (credit, billing plan, revenue schedule) and the technical track (tenant, single sign-on, licenses) start together. They meet at the license activation, which waits for the credit decision, and at the customer reply.

Nothing is written without a person

Claude and the Joule agent prepare. A person releases each step within their own limit. All numbers are computed from the sample data of one fictional order.
