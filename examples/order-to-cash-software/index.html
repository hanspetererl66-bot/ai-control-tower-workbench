One contract, six positions

Kestrel Versicherung AG buys three kinds of software (licences once, a monthly subscription with overage, software on consumption) and three services (implementation, training, managed operations).

The whole chain in 14 steps

Contract, order, bill of material, entitlement check, delivery, receipt by the customer, entitlement, technical provisioning, completion message, billing, consumption against billing, revenue, cash and revenue recognition. Use the bar at the top to run it step by step.

An n8n process calls the systems

CRM, order, material, entitlement, provisioning, billing and revenue are read in turn. The process decides nothing; a person decides at the entitlement deviations, the customer receipt and the consumption true-up.
