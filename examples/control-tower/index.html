DORA 2025: AI speed without test gates = instability
Why this happens: AI generates code faster than test coverage can keep pace. Engineers accept AI suggestions at 44% rate without a commensurate increase in automated test gates. At 30k engineers deploying daily, each 1pp of CFR = ~500 additional failed deployments per month. How long to fix and what it costs: Booking.com (DORA 2025) reduced AI-driven CFR by 40% within 6 months using mandatory automated test gates on AI-generated PRs.
