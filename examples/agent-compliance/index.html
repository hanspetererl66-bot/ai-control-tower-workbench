On track
Audit 12 Dec 2026
EU AI Act — Two distinct deadlines (Digital Omnibus, Jun 16 2026):
● Aug 2, 2026 (ACTIVE NOW): Transparency duties + GPAI model enforcement active. All AI systems interacting with humans must disclose AI nature. GPAI providers must maintain technical documentation. Applies to all Joule interactions and customer-facing agents.
● Dec 2, 2027 (EXTENDED): High-risk standalone Annex III agent conformity assessments. Original Aug 2026 deadline pushed 15 months. Finance, HR, and presales agents under Annex III now have structured runway to Dec 2027.
8 of 18 assessments complete. The remaining 10 are targeted before the 12 Dec 2026 external audit; the Dec 2027 statutory deadline is the fallback. Programme pivot: from audit sprint to embedded compliance process. The external audit on 12 Dec 2026 stays the nearest hard date; the statutory deadline gives a safety net, not a reason to slow down.
