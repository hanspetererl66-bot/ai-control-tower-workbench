One case, two assistants

Claude works at the desk across systems: it reads the emails, the delivery note and the contract and builds a case file where every statement carries its proof line. The Joule agent works inside S/4 under the signed-in person's rights.

Nothing is written without a person

Proposals wait in Proposals. A person releases each one, within their own limits. Neither Claude nor the Joule agent can release anything, and a credit memo above the clerk's limit needs the team lead.

Demo or real?

All numbers are computed from the sample data of one fictional case. With an S/4 connector and the Werkbank approval rules, the same steps would run on a real system. That connector is not part of this page.
