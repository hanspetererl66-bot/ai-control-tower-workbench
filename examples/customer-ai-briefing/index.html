What is this briefing?

A one-page briefing for the SAP account team about one end customer: which agents of the Autonomous Enterprise Suite (Finance and Supply Chain) the customer already uses, how heavily they are used, what has been bought or is being implemented, and what the AI-credit contract allows. Choose the customer at the top; everything below follows.

1. Where we stand

Three sentences and six numbers: agents live, in implementation, bought and planned, AI-unit use and the volume per day.

2. Agents and use

Every agent per area with its status, how autonomous it is and how much of its capacity is used. Click one for the detail.

3. Roadmap

When each agent went live or will go live, from purchased to planned, from July 2026 to September 2027.

4. Credits and contract

How many AI units are used per product, where the year-end lands, and which AI-credit contract type applies (marked with a check).

5. One picture

The briefing pulls together what is spread over several Spaces: contracts, open offers, open tickets and overdue invoices from Sub4Me, renewal dates from Renewals. The last card says where each block comes from.

    Autonomous Enterprise Suite: agents in use

    Utilisation is the share of the agent's monthly capacity that is used today; the consumption model behind each number is in the next card. “Handled without a person” is the share of cases an autonomous agent completes alone. Click a row for the detail.

    Suite packages and consumption

    The customer bought the Autonomous Enterprise Suite for Finance (5 agents) and for Supply Chain (8 agents). The agents are paid from the AI-unit contract: each agent counts one kind of event and uses a fixed number of AI units for each. AI units a month = units per event × events a month.

    SAP Business AI: BTP, Business Data Cloud and Joule Work

    The three components that consume AI units next to the agents. AI units are the common currency; Business Data Cloud also counts capacity units for its data services.

    Names as on SAP's public pricing pages: “SAP Business AI”, “SAP AI Units”, “SAP BTP Enterprise Agreement”, “SAP Business Data Cloud” with capacity units. “Joule Work” is the name used in the Sub4Me offer of this demo and is not checked against an SAP price list.
