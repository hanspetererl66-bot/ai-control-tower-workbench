What is this page?

How the picture is made

One customer group seen as a graph: companies, people, contracts, deals, orders, invoices, tickets and what people wrote. The facts live in text documents (one per node) that a knowledge engine (Cognee) reads. Each step of the story asks the documents one question; what comes back is lit up in the graph, with the lines that matched.

Retrieval here is local: a keyword ranking over the same documents, so the page works without a server. Switch on "Ask Cognee live" to send a question to the real Cognee memory through the app (needs the Cognee connector and the corpus; see docs/customer-360.md).

The "heat" of a company is a stated rule of thumb (payment record, renewal date, tickets, order gaps), not a model. Statements that are conclusions, not facts, carry the mark Inference. All data is fictional.
