# Procurement Handbook (example for the Ontology Manager demo)

Example text for the demo. It is invented and is not a real policy. It is meant to be read against the sample ontology
"Werkbank Procurement" (`werkbank_scm`) and the Finance Pre-Close ontology: some terms are already there, some are new.

## 1. Suppliers

A supplier is a company that delivers goods or services. Every supplier has a supplier number. A supplier scorecard rates
each supplier every quarter on on-time delivery and on quality. The scorecard is owned by Group Procurement.

## 2. Purchase orders

A purchase order is a binding order placed with one supplier. It has one or more purchase order lines, each with material,
quantity and price. A blanket order is a purchase order with an agreed total value that is called off in several deliveries.
Purchase orders above 25,000 in company code currency need approval by Group Procurement before they are sent.

## 3. Goods receipt

A goods receipt is the posting that ordered goods have arrived. It always refers to a purchase order line.
A goods receipt without an invoice becomes an accrual in the pre-close (see the Finance Closing Handbook).

## 4. Expediting

A purchase order line is late when the confirmed delivery date has passed and no goods receipt exists. Late lines are
expedited: the buyer contacts the supplier and records a new confirmed date. Lines that stay late for more than 14 days
are escalated to Group Procurement.

## 5. Metrics

On-time delivery rate is the share of purchase order lines whose goods receipt was posted on or before the confirmed
delivery date. It is certified by Group Procurement.
