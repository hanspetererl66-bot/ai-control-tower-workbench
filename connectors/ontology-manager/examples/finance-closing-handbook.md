# Finance Closing Handbook (example for the Ontology Manager demo)

Example text for the demo. It is invented for the Werkbank Finance Pre-Close baseline and is not a real group policy.
Company codes are DE10, FR10 and UK10, as in the Pre-Close space and the Databricks demo.

## 1. Purpose

The pre-close runs on working day 3 before the period is closed. Every company code completes the checks in this
handbook. Open items are cleared or accrued before close.

## 2. Accruals

An accrual is booked for every delivery that has been received but not yet invoiced. The accrual must not exceed the
open goods receipt / invoice receipt (GR/IR) balance of the purchase order. Each accrual names the supplier, the purchase
order and the company code it is booked in. Accruals above 50,000 in company code currency need approval by Group Controlling.

Margin after accruals is the margin adjusted for these accruals. It is certified by Group Controlling and is the
figure used in the margin deviation check.

## 3. GR/IR items

A GR/IR item is an open balance between goods received and invoices received for one purchase order line.
The supplier invoice and the purchase order must match before an item is cleared. Items open for more than
60 days are escalated to Group Procurement.

## 4. Intercompany differences

An intercompany difference arises when two group companies book the same delivery with different amounts.
Intercompany differences are cleared before close. The company code that delivered is responsible for the correction.
A difference above 10,000 is reported to Group Controlling on the same day.

## 5. Open purchase orders

A purchase order that is neither delivered nor invoiced counts towards the open purchase order exposure, which
is certified by Group Procurement. The supplier is named on every purchase order together with the delivery date and
the agreed payment terms.

## 6. Days sales outstanding

Days sales outstanding (DSO) is reviewed per company code. A DSO deviation of more than five days against the
previous period is explained in the close report. DSO is certified by Group Treasury.
