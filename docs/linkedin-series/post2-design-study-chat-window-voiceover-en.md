# Post 2 – Design Study: The chat window — spoken text (voice: Alicia)

## How Joule Work & Studio could evolve
Hi, I am Alicia, Design Engineer! Design Study twenty twenty-six: how Joule Work and Joule Studio could evolve, with exciting new features!
These are possible design proposals within an MVP. Not a finished product.

## The chat window
Welcome to the chat window of our design study. This is the stage for your next question.
Let us go down to the input field, and see what sits around it.

## Four tabs under the input
Right under the input field sit four tabs: Favorites, Suggested, Popular and What is new!
One click opens a tab, and another click closes it again!

## Popular, also from the organisation
Popular shows what you use most often. It can also draw on the ranking of your organisation.

## Hide the buttons
The little arrow next to the plus hides the tab buttons, so the input stays calm. Click it again, and they are back.

## Six fields: how Joule answers
And now the new context icon! It opens six fields. They steer how Joule answers, for the whole conversation.
Memory switches your saved memory on or off, and shows how many entries apply. Group adds a group's instructions and team memory. Persona sets tone and depth, for example short and number-focused for a controller. Space pulls in the memory entries tied to that Space.
What Joule knows is display only. It lists, section by section, what is sent with your next message. And Make skill drafts a reusable skill from the conversation. Nothing is saved, and it unlocks after two messages from you.

## Plus: material for one message
The plus on the left is Add context. Add up to twenty-five files: PowerPoint, Word, Excel, CSV, text and images, also by drag and drop. Or add a Jira ticket, a Notion page or a Slack thread by link or ID.
That is material for this one message. The six fields set the behaviour for the whole conversation.

## The microphone: just speak
And on the right, next to Send, sits the microphone!
Click it and speak, then click again. Your words are transcribed, and the text lands right in the input field, ready to send.
The recording goes to Deepgram, on a European endpoint. Only the text is kept, never the audio. You add your key once, in the settings.

## Closing
Three icons: one to add material, one to set how Joule answers, and the microphone, to simply speak!
