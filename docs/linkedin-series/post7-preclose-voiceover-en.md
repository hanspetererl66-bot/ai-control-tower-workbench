## Financial Pre-Close with Databricks

Hi, I am Grace, Design Engineer! Design Study twenty twenty-six: the Financial Pre-Close, with open items read from Databricks.

These are possible design proposals within an MVP. Sample data, not a finished product.

## One page for the days before close

One page for the last days before the books close. Revenue and EBIT forecast, with an eighty percent range.

Close readiness, open items, accruals to approve, and red alerts. A slider moves you through the working days.

## Forecast and budget bridge

The forecast evolution shows how the EBIT forecast moved every working day. The band narrows as the month fills.

And the bridge shows the biggest effects between budget and forecast.

## Profit and loss

The profit and loss lists booked, forecast with its range, budget, variance, and a status for every line. Click a line, and you see the method behind it.

## Company codes and close calendar

Company codes show readiness, open items and red alerts. Click one, and the whole page follows.

The close calendar draws one bar per task, from start to due working day, with who owns it, and what is blocked.

## Open items and reconciliations

What must be cleared before the cut-off, the largest first, with owner and next step. Intercompany differences sit on the side.

Prioritise with Joule, and it ranks them for you.

## Accrual proposals

Accrual proposals come with a basis, a confidence, and an amount. Estimate with TabPFN adds a likely final amount, and a correction risk.

They are proposals for the controller. Not accounting advice.

## From Databricks

Now the Databricks part. A press on Load from Databricks reads goods received and not yet invoiced, and intercompany balances, from the governed lakehouse, and checks them against SAP.

Four items to accrue. Two over ninety days, which need a review, not an accrual. One invoice before goods. And three intercompany differences.

Prepare accruals with Joule opens the conversation. Nothing is posted until you say yes there.

## Alerts and what if

Rules find alerts and anomalies in the postings. Ask Joule about each one.

And What if lets you tick risks and opportunities that are not in the forecast yet, and see the effect.

## Good to know

One honest note. The lakehouse holds synthetic demo data, and SAP is a mock. In this recording the Databricks answer is sample data, in the shape the connector returns. In the app, it comes from the connector.

## Closing

Forecast, open items and accruals in one view, with Databricks data. And you approve every posting.
