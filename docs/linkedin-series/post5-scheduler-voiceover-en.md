# Post 5 – Scheduler (voice: Camille, young, enthusiastic, light French accent; music: South African style)
Intro and outro as in the pre-trailer.

## Scheduler: everything on time

Hello! I am Camille, Design Engineer! Design Study twenty twenty-six: the Scheduler, one place to run every agent, workflow and skill on a timetable!

These are possible design proposals within an MVP. Sample data, not a finished product.

## All jobs in one list

Look at this! Forty-three jobs, and you see everything: what runs, when it runs next, when it ran last, the success rate, and the status!

An agent, a workflow, or a skill. All of them, side by side!

## Search, group, filter

At the top, search by name, by target, or by group. Then group the list, filter by status, and collapse or expand all, with one click!

## Group it your way

Group by functional type. Or by business area, like finance, sales or service. Or by organization unit!

And you can even add your own groupings, for example a project, or a cost center. Magnifique!

## Find what fails

Something is failing? Filter by failing, and there are your three jobs, in red!

Open one, and you get the run history: when it started, the trigger, the duration, the result, and a note about what went wrong.

## Create a job

Create a job in seconds! Pick a skill, a workflow or an agent. Add an instruction. Choose how often: every day, every week, monthly, once, or even a cron expression. And you see the next runs before you save!

## Run now, pause, delete

On every row: run now, pause, or delete. Need it right now? One click and it runs!

## Activity: the full log

The Activity tab is the history. Key numbers on top: forty-four runs, eighty-six percent succeeded, six failed, and the average time!

Then filter by time, result, type, or trigger, schedule or manual. And export everything as C S V!

## Good to know

One honest note. The Scheduler runs only while the app is open. A run that was missed is made up for, up to six hours. And the sample jobs are not logged in Activity, only your own runs.

## Closing

Search, group, filter. Create, run, pause. And a full activity log. Everything on time!
