## Chat settings and profiles

Hi, I am Lily, Design Engineer! Design Study twenty twenty-six: chat settings and working profiles.
These are possible design proposals within an MVP. Sample data, not a finished product.

## One icon, everything that goes along

Every chat has one small icon next to the message box. Open it, and you see everything that travels along with your question: memory, group, persona, space, and the working profile.
Plus the approval mode, and a button to turn a good conversation into a skill.

## Sent with every message

The side panel says it in plain words: what the assistant knows besides your question. Memory, instructions, working profile.
Each one can be switched off, or edited, right there.

## Language, length, tone

Under instructions: language, length and tone. Automatic, balanced and neutral by default.
Change them once, and they apply to every message.

## Working profiles

Now the working profile. A profile is a named package for one kind of work: the persona, the allowed connectors, preferred skills and spaces, which memory goes along, and how many steps the assistant may take.
Here, Sales. Changes need approval. Two connectors. All notes. Up to twenty steps.

## Review: read only

Or Review, read only. The assistant checks figures, contracts and risks, and refuses to change anything.
Only your style notes go along, and at most twelve steps.

## Only ever stricter

The important rule: a profile can only make the chat stricter. It never turns off an approval question, never grants a tool, and never overrides a safety rule.
Before you choose, you can see exactly what it adds. And you can save a copy as your own profile.

## Plain text, shareable

The text is plain. You can read the exact instruction that goes to the assistant.
Profiles can be exported and imported, with a preview and warnings first. Up to ten of your own, and admins can publish them to groups.

## Personas and own commands

In the settings, the chat tab holds personas and your own slash commands. Sales, controlling and executive come built in.
A command like slash briefing is replaced by its prepared question before the message is sent. Nothing runs by itself.

## Memory, on your terms

Memory keeps three kinds of notes: about me, how I like results, and projects. Nothing is saved without your OK, and it stays on this computer.
Language, length, tone and standing instructions are set here as well.

## Data and audit

Data and audit. Export everything, or delete it. And an audit trail shows who asked to move a skill, a memory entry or an agent to a higher level, and who decided.

## Good to know

One honest note. Everything here is sample data, and it runs on one computer. A real roll-out needs a server with separate approvers.
Profiles narrow what the assistant does. A person still decides.

## Closing card

Choose the work. Set the rules. A person decides.
