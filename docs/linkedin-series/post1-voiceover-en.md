# Post 1 – Design Study 2026: spoken text (voice: Ben)

## How Joule Work & Studio could evolve
Hi, I am Ben! Design Study twenty twenty-six: how Joule Work and Joule Studio could evolve, with exciting new features!
These are possible design proposals within an MVP. Not a finished product.

## The overview: rail, list and chat
Let us start with the overview. On the far left, the narrow bar is the rail.
Right next to it, the list of your conversations.
And on the right, the chat window, where you work with Joule.
Back on the rail, from top to bottom: Conversations, your chats.
Spaces, your work organized by domain.
Develop, for workflows and agents.
Further down, the Scheduler, to run agents and workflows on a schedule.
Admin, with the ontologies and the registry.
And at the very bottom: help and shortcuts, your settings, and your profile.

## New: a chat with four tabs
New: the chat starts with four tabs! Favorites, Suggested, Popular, and What’s new.

## New: Spaces by domain
Spaces are small dashboards, grouped by domain. You find them right in the left menu.

## New: Subspaces & drag and drop
Spaces come with subspaces: categories and subcategories.
Drag a Space onto another one, and it moves in as a subspace. With one single mouse gesture!

## New: Change with Joule
And every Space can be changed with Joule, right from the top!

## New: Develop
Develop guides you in five steps: intent, requirements, specification, build, and test and go live.

## New: the Scheduler
New: the Scheduler. Look for the calendar icon in the menu.
Agents, workflows or skills, daily, weekly, monthly, or by cron.

## New: Personal settings
New: personal settings. Skins, accent colors, fonts and density, all in one place.
Pick a skin, and the whole app, and every Space, follows!

## Closing
New features at a glance: chat tabs, Spaces and subspaces, Change with Joule, Develop, the Scheduler, and personal settings. A design study.
