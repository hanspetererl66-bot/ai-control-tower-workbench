## The renewal radar

The renewal radar shows every contract against its decision date: the contract end, minus the notice period. Nothing slips by quietly.

## Why this colour

Click a renewal, and you see why it has its colour. Here, a price rise of eighty percent, against a contract cap of three.
The customer mail is right there, and the next actions. Automatic renewal is only offered for small contracts, and only if you switch it on. Otherwise, a person handles it.

## Good to know

Joule prepares and checks. It never sends, prices, or signs. A person does.
