# Post 3 – Spaces (updated: eight layouts, new Spaces)

Narrator: Alicia. Format 4:5, English. Design study with sample data, not an SAP product.

## Spaces: your own pages in Joule

- Hi, I am Alicia, Design Engineer! Design Study twenty twenty-six: Spaces, your own pages inside Joule Work and Joule Studio, with ideas of future features!
- These are possible design and feature proposals within an MVP. Not a finished product.

## Spaces: pages for your work

- A Space is a page inside the product, for sales, finance or operations, grouped in domains.

## Organise, search, duplicate

- Drag a Space onto a domain, or onto another Space, to nest it. Search finds a Space by name or by description.
- One click on the copy icon duplicates a Space.

## Inside a Space

- Open a Space, and you get a real working page: key figures, tickets, offers and charts, in one view.

## The header of every Space

- In the header: search inside this page. A text size slider, that applies to all Spaces. Insights, and Share, to send a Space as a file.
- Describe in plain words what should change, and Joule does the work.

## Eight layouts, one click

- In settings, under Look, you pick one of eight layouts for the whole app, and for every Space.
- Minimal, Structural, Contemporary, Aurora, Clay, Kinetic, Tactile and Assistant. Same data, a different shape.

## New: design a Space, no code

- Another idea is the new Space designer! Click Design a Space, and build a page from blocks.
- Start blank, or from a template.

## Sixteen building blocks

- Sixteen building blocks: headings, key figures, sortable tables, charts, progress bars, steps, a timeline and an Ask Joule button.
- Click a block to edit it. Type the numbers, or paste cells straight from a spreadsheet.

## Change with Joule, in the designer

- The designer has Change with Joule too. Joule proposes a new page, and shows what it adds and changes.
- Nothing happens until you apply it. And Undo brings the old page back.

## Save, share, edit again

- Save, and the Space joins your list. Share it as a file, and edit the design again whenever you like.
- One honest note: the numbers are the ones you enter. Live data comes later.

## New Spaces: 360, hiring, graph

- New Spaces: a Customer 360 as a three-D graph with a story in four acts, a hiring approval chain, and a sales knowledge graph.

## Closing
- Organise, search, duplicate. Eight layouts. New Spaces. And a designer with sixteen blocks, to build your own Space!
