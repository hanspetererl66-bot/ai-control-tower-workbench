## n8n dashboard and Admin console

Hi, I am Leo, Design Engineer! Design Study twenty twenty-six: the n8n Performance Dashboard, and the Admin console with registry, ontologies and skill management.

These are possible design proposals within an MVP. Sample data, not a finished product.

## n8n at a glance

First, the n8n Performance Dashboard. Ten of eleven workflows are active, with fourteen hundred executions in two weeks.

Success rate ninety-four percent, against a target of ninety-eight. Three workflows are below target, and the dashboard says so, right on top.

## Every workflow, one row

Every workflow in one row: category, project, trigger, the apps it touches, runs, success, a trend, and the last status. Search it, or filter by category.

## Errors and connections

The Errors tab shows which apps the failing workflows have in common. Here: HTTP, Gmail and Slack. That tells you where to look first.

Connections shows which apps your workflows depend on, and which app is a single point to watch.

## Schedules and capacity

Schedules shows when workflows start, by hour, and by trigger.

Capacity compares your runs with the plan limit. About a third of the monthly limit is used, and the projection says where you will end up.

## LLM cost, builders, governance

LLM cost estimates what the workflows with a model node cost. Builders shows who built what, by project.

And governance checks every workflow against six rules: owner, project, error workflow, category, review, and runs.

## The Admin console

Now the Admin console. Three tiles, three questions. What does every term mean? What was built? And which skills are in good shape?

## Ontologies

Ontologies: the shared vocabulary of your agents in one place. Every term has a definition, an owner, and links to what depends on it.

Classes, properties, certified metrics, and the proposals waiting for a decision.

## Solution register

The Solution register lists every agent, workflow and feature, with owner, lifecycle phase, criticality, and whether a person has verified the entry.

Open one, and you see the register card: ownership, lifecycle, governance, connections, and the last thirty days.

## Skill management

And skill management: the health of all skills, duplicates, and phase-out proposals.

Every proposal waits for a person to confirm. Nothing happens by itself.

## Good to know

One honest note. The dashboard shows fictional sample data, and the costs are estimates. Live data would come from your n8n Cloud instance, and only names, tags, status and timings are read, never the payloads. The register and the ontology show example items.

## Closing

See how n8n performs. Know every term, every solution, and every skill. A person decides.
