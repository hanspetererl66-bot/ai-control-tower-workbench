## Where agents begin

Hi, I am Marcus, Design Engineer! Design Study twenty twenty-six: Jira, Notion, Slack and Teams, as the start of agentic engineering.
These are possible design proposals within an MVP. Sample data, not a finished product.

## Requirements already exist

Requirements rarely start in a new tool. They sit in Jira tickets, Slack threads, Teams channels and Notion pages.
So the process starts there. Develop, tile zero: start from requirements.

## Jira: read, not write

Pick a source, and say where to read. For Jira, a project key or a query. Joule loads the tickets.
Read only. Nothing is changed in Jira.

## Slack threads

Slack works the same way. A channel, a thread link, or search words.
Joule keeps the messages that state a need, and skips the chatter.

## Microsoft Teams

In Microsoft Teams, name the team and the channel, or paste the link to a message.
The app needs permission to read channel messages. Again, read only.

## Notion pages

And Notion: a page, a database, or search words.
Share the pages with the integration first, and only those are read.

## One list, any source

Everything lands in one list, whatever the source. You tick the requirements that belong together.
The ones you do not tick are still checked: Joule tells you if one belongs to a cluster.

## Cluster and classify

Joule clusters them by topic, and proposes a class for each: agent, workflow, an extension, or a feature. With a confidence, and the reason in plain words.
Four clusters from eight requirements. And a suggestion: this ticket you did not tick may belong here. Add it, or not.

## Count the voices

When several people ask for the same thing, the cluster shows the mentions, per source. Seven here: two in Jira, five in Slack.
The agent does the counting, so the quotes are one click away. You check them, and you can change the class.

## Confirm, then hand over

Before anything leaves for Jira, you see the text. A label, and one comment, on the tickets you choose. Nothing else is touched.
Only then comes the hand-over: into Agent Studio, the workflow wizard, or the registry.

## Good to know

One honest note. These are sample tickets and sample messages. The classes are proposals, and a person changes them.
Joule reads and clusters. It writes a note to Jira only after you confirm. A person decides.
