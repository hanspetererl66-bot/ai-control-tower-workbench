Intro and outro as in the pre-trailer; the cat dances upright with top hat, cane and black shoes.

## Skill management: keep skills in shape

Good day. I am Walter, Design Engineer. Design Study twenty twenty-six: Skill management. How a company keeps its skills in good shape.

These are possible design proposals within an MVP. Sample data, not a finished product.

## In the Admin console

In the Admin console you find Skill management, next to the Ontologies and the Solution register.

It answers three questions. Which skills are in use? Which are duplicated? And which ones does nobody look after?

## The overview

The overview: seventy skills in the catalog. Almost a third are duplicates, and nearly two thirds are rarely used, or not at all.

Bars show the health of every skill, the numbers by department, and the most and the least used skills.

## Proposals, not decisions

Thirty proposals are waiting: merge duplicates, phase out what nobody uses.

Each one is only a suggestion. Nothing happens until a person confirms it, and every action goes into the log.

## Duplicates

Skills that do almost the same are grouped by similarity. Pick the one to keep, and the others are phased out and point to it. Or say: these are not duplicates.

## The catalog

The catalog lists every skill, with state, owner, level and runs. Filter, sort, and select several at once.

Phase out, retire, restore, mark as reviewed, ask the owner to confirm, request the next level, or give it a new owner.

## The policy

The policy sets the rules. Unused after ninety days. Rarely used below three runs. The owner confirms every hundred and eighty days. And a thirty day phase-out.

Every change is recorded in the log.

## Three levels, one approval each

In Develop, a skill lives on one of three levels: personal, department, or company. It moves up one level at a time, and a skill never skips a level.

First an automatic check: secrets, scripts, tools that change data. Then the department lead decides, or the governance board, for the whole company.

## Good to know

One honest note. The numbers are invented sample data. The shared database exists only in the desktop app. And the approvals here are a simulation, where one person plays every role.

## Closing

Find duplicates, phase out the unused, and move good skills up with an approval. A person decides.
